Artifact Information

Title: Explaining Neural Networks in Simple Language

Purpose: To explain neural networks in a simple and understandable way by using everyday examples that are familiar to a non-technical audience.

Audience: Family members, students, business professionals, and others who do not have a background in computer science or machine learning.

This artifact was created as part of my graduate coursework in Machine Learning Fundamentals. It explains how neural networks learn from data by comparing the process to a child learning to recognize a dog. It also provides practical examples such as Face Unlock, email spam detection, and video recommendations.

Use of Generative AI

I used ChatGPT as a learning and writing support tool while developing this artifact. ChatGPT helped me create an initial explanation of neural networks for a non-technical audience. I reviewed the response, revised the content, and rewrote the explanation in my own words based on my understanding of the topic.

Intended Audience

This artifact is intended for people who do not have a background in machine learning or computer science. The explanation uses simple language and everyday examples so that both technical and non-technical audiences can understand the basic idea of how neural networks work.

Reflection

Working on this artifact helped me understand that explaining a complex technical topic in simple language requires a clear understanding of the concept. I learned that using familiar examples, such as a child learning to recognize a dog, can make neural networks easier for others to understand.

This activity also improved my communication skills and helped me learn how to present technical information to a non-technical audience. It showed me that technical knowledge is important, but the ability to explain that knowledge clearly is also valuable in professional and business environments.
