Artifact Information

Title: Personal Framework: Seeing Myself as an AI/ML Change Leader

Introduction: This artifact reflects my professional growth throughout this course and how my understanding of AI/ML has expanded beyond the technical aspects of developing AI systems.

Description: This personal framework identifies my strengths, areas for improvement, leadership values, professional goals, and action plans for continuing my growth as an AI/ML professional and future change leader.

Objective: To develop a personal framework that connects my technical background with leadership, communication, ethics, responsible AI, and change management.

Process: I began with a personal assessment to identify my strengths and areas for growth. Throughout the course, I reflected on what I learned about AI/ML, ethics, bias, privacy, transparency, and organizational change. I then used these experiences to create my mission statement, core values, objectives, action plans, and evaluation process.

Tools and Technology Used: Personal assessment, course assignments and discussions, Microsoft Word, and ChatGPT as a writing and grammar support tool.

Value Proposition of the Artifact

Unique Value: This artifact demonstrates my ability to connect technical AI/ML knowledge with leadership and organizational change. It shows that I understand successful AI implementation requires more than technology. Communication, collaboration, ethics, accountability, and the ability to help people adapt to change are also important.

Relevance: As organizations continue to adopt AI technologies, professionals need to understand both the technical and human side of AI implementation. This framework demonstrates my commitment to developing the leadership and communication skills needed to support responsible and effective AI adoption.

Use of Generative AI

I used ChatGPT to help improve grammar, sentence structure, and organization. The ideas, personal reflections, strengths, areas for improvement, and professional goals are based on my own assessment and learning throughout the course.

Intended Audience

This artifact is intended for technology professionals, recruiters, managers, and organizations interested in AI/ML professionals who understand both the technical and organizational aspects of AI adoption. It is also relevant to individuals interested in responsible AI, leadership development, and change management.

Reflection

Creating this artifact helped me reflect on how my understanding of AI/ML has changed throughout this course. At the beginning, I focused more on the technical side of AI, such as models and data. Throughout the course, I learned that successful AI implementation also requires consideration of ethics, transparency, bias, privacy, communication, and how AI can affect people and organizations.

This activity also helped me identify areas where I want to continue improving, especially communication, leadership, project management, and change management. As I continue developing my technical AI/ML knowledge, I also want to strengthen my ability to work with different stakeholders and help organizations adopt AI in a responsible and effective way.
