Title: Personal Framework: Seeing Myself as an AI/ML Change Leader

Purpose: To reflect on my personal and professional growth throughout this course and develop a framework for becoming a leader who can support the responsible and effective adoption of AI and machine learning in organizations.

Audience: Technology professionals, recruiters, managers, business leaders, and anyone interested in AI/ML leadership, responsible AI, and organizational change.

The framework includes my mission statement, core values, objectives, action plans, and plans for evaluating my continued professional growth.

Reflection

Working on this artifact helped me reflect on how my understanding of AI and machine learning has changed throughout this course. At the beginning, I mainly focused on the technical side of AI, such as models and data. Throughout the course, I learned that successful AI implementation also requires consideration of ethics, bias, privacy, transparency, communication, and how AI can affect people and organizations.

This activity also helped me identify areas where I want to continue improving, especially communication, leadership, project management, and change management. As I continue developing my technical AI/ML knowledge, I also want to strengthen my ability to work with different stakeholders and help organizations adopt AI in a responsible and effective way.
